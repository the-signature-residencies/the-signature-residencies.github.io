Built to last

External

01Construction
Earthquake-resistant, strong RCC frame structure.
02Security
24x7 CCTV cameras from a standard company across the campus.
03Entrance foyer
Vitrified flooring, professionally designed ceiling, reception table and chair, and name board.
04Terrace
Waterproofing with china mosaic.
05Lifts
ISI-standard lifts with automatic doors and safety devices.
06Plaster
Double-coat plaster outside; mala plaster inside.
07Parking
Parking on the ground floor and two basements.
08Generator
An automatic generator for the common areas of every building.

Internal

01Flooring
Granamite or vitrified tile flooring in the living room, kitchen and bedrooms.
02Electrical
ISI-standard concealed wiring with modular switchboards.
03Doors
An elegant main door with a wooden frame; flush doors with granite frames inside.
04Kitchen
Platform with stainless-steel sink; glazed-tile dado up to lintel level.
05Windows
Aluminium-section windows with frames.
06Plumbing
ISI-standard UPVC and CPVC concealed fittings.
07Staircase
Granite treads on the stairs; vitrified tiles in the passage.
